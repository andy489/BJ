Know the game before you play
Blackjack
Decks
2-deck shoe
Blackjack pays
3 : 2
Ace value
1 or 11
Face cards
J, Q, K, 10 → 10
Bust
Hand over 21 — wager lost
Push
Tie — bet returned
Min bet
$10.00
Max bet
$1,000.00

Player Actions
Hit
Draw one more card
Stand
Keep your current hand
Double Down
Double the bet, receive exactly one more card
Split
Split a pair into two separate hands, each with a new bet
Surrender
Fold and recover half your bet
Insurance
Side bet offered when dealer shows an Ace — pays 2:1
Even Money
Instant 1:1 payout when you hold Blackjack and dealer shows Ace
Betting Panel
Repeat
Re-place your last wager exactly
Double
Double your last wager before dealing
Clear
Remove chips from the betting circle
