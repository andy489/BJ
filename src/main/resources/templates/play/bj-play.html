Double Down
Your bet is doubled after the first two cards. You receive exactly one more card.
Outcome Pays Example (bet 10+10)

Insurance & Even Money
Available when the dealer shows an Ace. Insurance costs half your main bet. Even Money is offered when you hold Blackjack.

Perfect Pairs (PP)
Based on your first two cards only. Settled before the hand plays out.
Hand Pays Example (bet 10)
Perfect Pair — same rank, same suit 30 : 1bet 10 → return 310
Coloured Pair — same rank, same colour10 : 1bet 10 → return 110
Mixed Pair — same rank, different colour 5 : 1 bet 10 → return 60
No pair Loss bet 10 → return 0
Dealer Perfect Pairs (DPP)
Same rules as Perfect Pairs, applied to the dealer's first two cards. Same paytable.

21+3
Based on your first two cards plus the dealer's up-card forming a poker hand.
Hand Pays Example (bet 10)
Suited Three-of-a-Kind — same rank, same suit100 : 1bet 10 → return 1010
Straight Flush — same suit, consecutive ranks 40 : 1 bet 10 → return 410
Three-of-a-Kind — same rank, any suits 30 : 1 bet 10 → return 310

RTP figures (PP 96.15%, 21+3 99.18%) are computed analytically assuming an infinite deck (cards drawn with replacement — each draw is independent). With a finite number of decks the RTP is lower: e.g. 86.82% / 95.38% for 6 decks.
How to Play
What every button on the table does
Betting Phase

Place your bet before dealing. Chips go to whichever bet area is currently selected (main hand or a side-bet circle).

ButtonWhat it does
Chips (2.5 – 100) Add that chip value to the currently selected bet area (main hand or the highlighted side-bet circle).
Repeat Add the previous hand's main-hand bet and side bets on top of any chips already staged.
Double (bet) Double your current main hand bet. Does not affect side bets.
Clear Clears the currently selected bet area and returns those chips to your balance.
If the selected area is already £0, one click clears all bet areas (main hand + all side bets) at once.
DealStart the hand. Locks in all bets and deals two cards each.
Side-Bet Circles (PP / 21+3 / DPP)

The three oval circles on the table are both bet displays and chip-target selectors.

ActionEffect
Click an unselected circle Routes subsequent chip clicks to that side bet. The circle is highlighted to show it is the active target.
Click the already-selected circle Deselects it — chip target returns to the main hand.
PP — Perfect PairsWins when your first two cards share the same rank. Higher payouts for matching colour (Coloured) or identical suit (Perfect).
21+3Wins when your two cards + the dealer's up-card form a poker hand (Flush, Straight, Three-of-a-Kind, Straight Flush, or Suited Three-of-a-Kind).
DPP — Dealer Perfect PairsSame as PP but evaluated on the dealer's first two cards.
Decision Phase

Once cards are dealt, choose your action. Greyed-out buttons are unavailable in the current situation.

ButtonWhat it does
Hit Draw one more card. You can hit multiple times. Busting (going over 21) loses the hand immediately.
If your current score is 17 or higher, a confirmation prompt appears before the card is drawn.
Stand Take no more cards and end your turn. The dealer then plays.
If your hard score is 11 or lower (and the hand is not soft), a confirmation prompt appears before standing.
Double Double your bet and receive exactly one more card, then stand automatically. Only available on your first two cards.
Split Split a matching pair into two separate hands, each with its own equal bet. Only available when your first two cards share the same rank.
If your pair rank is lower than the dealer's up-card rank (weak-pair scenario), a confirmation prompt appears first.
Surrender Forfeit the hand and recover half your bet. Only available as your very first action before any other move.
Always requires confirmation via a prompt before the hand is forfeited.
Auto Play Let the computer finish the hand using Dealer-Mirror strategy (hit ≤16, stand ≥17).
Greyed-out action buttons mean that move is not available right now — e.g. Split requires a matching pair, Double is only offered on the opening two cards, and Surrender must be your first action.
